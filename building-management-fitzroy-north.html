What it covers

Building management without a full time manager on site.

A building of 60 to 90 lots may not need a building manager on site every day. Its lifts, fire systems, access control, common areas and contractors still need regular attention. Part time building management gives these buildings that oversight, with contractor supervision, written reporting and a reliable point of contact.

  1. Contractors

    What the committee needs
    The right contractors, properly coordinated, with their work checked.
    What we do
    We source, brief, supervise and quality check contractors, meet them on site and follow up outstanding work. Every contractor signs in digitally on arrival, so attendance is on record.

  2. Compliance

    What the committee needs
    Inspections, servicing and compliance records kept on track.
    What we do
    We manage essential safety measures, fire system servicing, OHS and regulatory tracking, and keep records so the committee can see what is done and what needs a decision.

  3. Open items

    What the committee needs
    Nothing left to drift between committee meetings.
    What we do
    Open items are tracked on a written works register, followed through to completion and reported to the committee in writing.

  4. Quotes and invoices

    What the committee needs
    Knowing what the owners corporation is paying for.
    What we do
    Visits are met on site and compared against the invoice, and quotes are questioned before they are recommended.

  5. Residents

    What the committee needs
    A reliable point of contact for everyday building matters.
    What we do
    Resident communications and common property inspections, so issues are found early.

  6. Emergencies

    What the committee needs
    Support when something goes wrong between site visits.
    What we do
    24/7 emergency response.

Terms

Straightforward terms for owners corporations.

Committees should know exactly what they are agreeing to before they appoint a building manager.

Six month trial

Every JKFM building management agreement starts with a six month trial period.

Your committee judges the work before the term means anything.

No security interests

JKFM does not take security interests over owners corporation assets or funds. Some service contracts in the market include PPSR registration rights, break fees and penalty interest; committees should check theirs.

Written reports

A written report to every committee meeting covering completed work, open items, upcoming maintenance, identified risks and financial summaries.
