01

The founder

Meet the founder.

  1. 01.1Who he is

    Karan Sood is the Director of JK Facilities Management. Based in Melbourne, he founded JKFM in 2023 with a straightforward goal: to bring structure, clarity, and accountability to an industry that often lacks all three. He is the hands on manager of JKFM's flagship building in South Yarra. The person who signs the contract is the person who knows the building.

  2. 01.2What he has seen

    With hands on experience managing residential buildings for owners corporations, Karan understands the day to day realities that committees face. From chasing contractors who disappear after quoting, to making sense of maintenance reports that raise more questions than they answer, to dealing with cleaning that looks fine until you check the bin room.

    Before founding JKFM, Karan worked across property and facilities management in Melbourne, developing a deep understanding of strata legislation, building compliance requirements, and the operational challenges that come with managing residential apartment buildings. He saw the same problems everywhere: reactive maintenance that costs more in the long run, contractors who operate without oversight, and committees left to coordinate things they shouldn't have to.

  3. 01.3How that shaped JKFM

    That experience shaped everything about how JKFM operates. Each engagement starts with a structured management plan. Every contractor is supervised and quality checked. Every committee receives written status reports, not verbal updates that get forgotten. If something happens in your building, there's a documented trail.

  4. 01.4The belief it rests on

    Committees deserve a building manager who treats their building as if it were his own.

    That means catching problems early, holding contractors accountable, and giving committees the clear, honest information they need to make good decisions. That philosophy drives every decision at JKFM.

02

The approach

From reactive chaos to proactive control.

A philosophy that changes how buildings are managed.

  1. 02.1Structured management

    Every engagement runs to a documented management plan with clear priorities, timelines, and responsibilities. Nothing is left to memory or guesswork.

  2. 02.2Documented processes

    From contractor sign ins to cleaning checklists, every process is recorded. If it happened, there's a paper trail.

  3. 02.3Clear reporting

    Committees receive written status updates that cover open items, completed work, and upcoming maintenance. Not vague summaries or verbal updates that get forgotten.

  4. 02.4Committee empowerment

    We handle the operational complexity so committees can focus on what matters: informed decision making. Your committee stays in control. We make that possible.

03

Values

Our values.

The principles JKFM works to, stated in order.

  1. 03.1Transparency

    Clear reporting, open communication, no hidden agendas. Committees see exactly what's happening in their building, the good and the bad, so they can make informed decisions.

  2. 03.2Accountability

    Everything is documented. Contractor attendance, cleaning quality, maintenance progress. If we say it's done, there's a record to prove it. We hold ourselves to the same standard we hold our contractors.

  3. 03.3Proactive care

    We catch issues early, before they become costly failures. Regular inspections, preventative maintenance, and risk identification mean fewer surprises and lower long term costs for your owners corporation.

  4. 03.4Reliability

    Consistent, structured, dependable. Committees can count on regular updates, scheduled inspections, and a building manager who follows through. No chasing required.

  2. 04.2Clear communication

  3. 04.3Committee empowerment
