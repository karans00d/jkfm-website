About JKFM

Proactive building management and cleaning services for owners corporations across Melbourne. Founded on accountability, driven by results.

Meet the Founder

Karan Sood is the Director of JK Facilities Management. Based in Melbourne, he founded JKFM with a straightforward goal: to bring structure, clarity, and accountability to an industry that often lacks all three.

With hands-on experience managing residential buildings for owners corporations, Karan understands the day-to-day realities that committees face. From chasing contractors who disappear after quoting, to making sense of maintenance reports that raise more questions than they answer, to dealing with cleaning that looks fine until you check the bin room.

Before founding JKFM, Karan worked across property and facilities management in Melbourne, developing a deep understanding of strata legislation, building compliance requirements, and the operational challenges that come with managing multi-unit residential buildings. He saw the same problems everywhere: reactive maintenance that costs more in the long run, contractors who operate without oversight, and committees left to coordinate things they shouldn't have to.

That experience shaped everything about how JKFM operates. Every building gets a structured management plan. Every contractor is supervised and quality-checked. Every committee receives written status reports, not verbal updates that get forgotten. If something happens in your building, there's a documented trail.

Karan's approach is built on a simple belief: committees deserve a building manager who treats their building as if it were his own. That means catching problems early, holding contractors accountable, and giving committees the clear, honest information they need to make good decisions. That philosophy drives every decision at JKFM.

Our Values

The principles behind every building we manage.

01

Transparency

Clear reporting, open communication, no hidden agendas. Committees see exactly what's happening in their building — the good and the bad — so they can make informed decisions.

02

Accountability

Everything is documented. Contractor attendance, cleaning quality, maintenance progress — if we say it's done, there's evidence to prove it. We hold ourselves to the same standard we hold our contractors.

03

Proactive Care

We catch issues early, before they become costly failures. Regular inspections, preventative maintenance, and risk identification mean fewer surprises and lower long-term costs for your owners corporation.

04

Reliability

Consistent, structured, dependable. Committees can count on regular updates, scheduled inspections, and a building manager who follows through. No chasing required.

Why Choose JKFM
