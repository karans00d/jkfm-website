# Findings: JKFM Sales & Marketing Plan

## Target Market Research

### Victorian Strata Industry
- Strata/OC market in Victoria is large and regulated under the Owners Corporations Act
- OC committees are NOT publicly listed — Victorian OC Register shows building addresses only
- Decision makers: Individual strata managers at management firms (NOT firm CEOs)
- Strata managers recommend or directly appoint FM/cleaning contractors
- Pain point: Strata managers hate complaints — FM providers who reduce complaints win loyalty

### Known Strata Management Firms in Victoria
- PICA Group
- Ace Body Corporate Management
- Whittles
- Strata Plus
- Body Corporate Services
- Stratacare Australia
- Australian Strata Management
- SCA Victoria (Strata Community Association — industry body, good for networking)

### Finding Individual Strata Managers
- Firm websites → "Our Team" pages
- LinkedIn search: "strata manager" + "Victoria"
- Build list of 30–50 individual managers to start

## JKFM Website Analysis
- Strong positioning: "Moving from Reactive Chaos to Proactive Control"
- Three pillars: Risk-Led Oversight | Contractor Accountability | Clear Reporting
- Has services page, cleaning page, contact/quote form
- Hero stats shown: 150+ properties, 10+ years experience, 98% satisfaction, 24/7 response
- Good foundation for linking in outreach emails

## Reference Client (Existing)
- 111-lot residential building
- Provides both building management + cleaning services
- Can be referenced in outreach as social proof (with permission)

## Outreach Sequence Decided
Per strata manager:
| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|

| Day 4 | Follow-up email if no reply |
