Room by room

What your property manager checks, and what we clean.

When you move out, your property manager compares the apartment with the original condition report, allowing for fair wear and tear. Our end of lease clean focuses on the areas most often checked at the final inspection.

  1. Kitchen

    What gets checked
    Grease, food residue and marks left by everyday use, on the rangehood, inside the oven and inside cupboards.
    What we clean
    Oven, rangehood and cupboards cleaned inside and out.

  2. Bathrooms

    What gets checked
    Soap scum, water marks and build up in the shower and on the vanity.
    What we clean
    Shower, toilet, vanity and mirror cleaned.

  3. Bedrooms

    What gets checked
    Dust and marks left behind once the furniture is gone, and stains on the carpet.
    What we clean
    Each bedroom cleaned and the carpet steam cleaned.

  4. Living areas

    What gets checked
    Floors, carpets and marks left by furniture and foot traffic.
    What we clean
    Living and dining areas cleaned, carpet steam cleaned where there is carpet.

  5. Carpets

    What gets checked
    Built up dirt, general soiling and stains across every carpeted room.
    What we clean
    Carpet steam cleaning in every carpeted room, included in the price, so there is no separate contractor to book. Only need the carpets? See carpet steam cleaning.
