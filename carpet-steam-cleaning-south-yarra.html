When to book

When apartment carpets need a steam clean.

Vacuuming removes surface dust and loose dirt, but not what sits deeper in the carpet fibres. A steam clean lifts built up dirt and refreshes the carpet. These are the times residents usually book one.

  1. Moving out

    What happens
    Carpets are checked against the condition report at the final inspection, allowing for fair wear and tear.
    What we do
    Steam cleaned at $66 a room, or included in our end of lease clean if you need the whole apartment done.

  2. Moving in

    What happens
    Even when an apartment looks clean, carpets can hold dirt left by the last occupant.
    What we do
    Steam cleaned while the rooms are still empty, before your furniture goes in.

  3. Pets, spills and stains

    What happens
    Pets, food spills and daily use leave marks and odours in the fibres.
    What we do
    Steam cleaned room by room. Some stains and odours are permanent, especially where they have soaked into the backing.

  4. Once or twice a year

    What happens
    Living rooms, bedrooms and hallways collect dirt even with regular vacuuming.
    What we do
    A routine steam clean, booked for one room or the whole apartment.
