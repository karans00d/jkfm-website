Where it builds up

What builds up, and what we clean.

Car parks, bin rooms, driveways and other shared areas take vehicle traffic, dirt, spills and water runoff. Without regular cleaning, grease and grime build up on the surfaces, and leaves, litter and silt block the drainage channels.

  1. Spoon drains and grates

    What builds up
    Spoon drains are the shallow concrete channels along car park floors, ramps and driveways that carry water to the drain pits. Silt, leaves and litter settle in them, and a blocked spoon drain sends water across the floor.
    What we do
    We remove the debris, clear and wash out the spoon drains, and clean and refit removable grates.

  2. Car park floors and ramps

    What builds up
    Dust, tyre marks and oil from daily vehicle movements.
    What we do
    Concrete car park floors, ramps and vehicle access areas degreased and pressure washed.

  3. Bin rooms

    What builds up
    Leaking waste, food residue, spills and smells around the bins and floor.
    What we do
    Floors and surfaces pressure washed and deodorised.

  4. Paths, entries and driveways

    What builds up
    Foot traffic, weather and everyday dirt at the building's front door.
    What we do
    Paths, entries and driveways pressure washed, as a one off or as part of a regular schedule.

  5. Balconies

    What builds up
    Dust and grime on balcony tiles and outdoor surfaces.
    What we do
    Balconies cleaned for residents, where the building's rules and drainage allow it.
