Privacy policy.

JK Facilities Management (ABN 78 670 382 420) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our website or services.

01

Collection

Information we collect.

  1. 01.1When we collect it

    We may collect personal information when you:

    • a.Submit a contact or quote request form on our website
    • b.Communicate with us by email or phone
    • c.Engage us for facilities management or cleaning services

  2. 01.2What it may include

    This may include your name, email address, phone number, property address, and details about your enquiry or building.

02

Use

How we use your information.

  1. 02.1Purposes

    We use your information to:

    • a.Respond to your enquiries and provide quotes
    • b.Deliver and manage our services
    • c.Communicate with you about your building or property
    • d.Improve our services and website

03

Disclosure

Disclosure.

  1. 03.1No sale of information

    We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with contractors or service providers who assist us in delivering our services, where necessary and under confidentiality obligations.

04

Security

Security.

  1. 04.1Reasonable steps

    We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access. However, no internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

05

Access

Access and correction.

  1. 05.1Your rights

    You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, or request corrections. Contact us at enquiries@jkfacilitiesmanagement.com.au or 0459 361 650.

06

Contact

Contact.

  1. 06.1Questions about this policy

    For questions about this Privacy Policy or our handling of your information, contact:
