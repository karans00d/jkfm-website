Terms

Terms your committee will ask about.

  1. A six month trial period

    Every JKFM building management agreement starts with a six month trial period. Your committee judges the work before the term means anything.

  2. No security interests

    JKFM does not take security interests over owners corporation assets or funds. Some service contracts in the market include PPSR registration rights, break fees and penalty interest; committees should check theirs.

  3. Written reporting

    A written report to every committee meeting covering completed work, open items, upcoming maintenance, identified risks and financial summaries.

Working with us

What you get as the manager.

  1. One accountable party

    JKFM can hold both the building management and cleaning contracts, as it does at its flagship building. Nothing falls between two contractors, and when something needs chasing you make one call.

  2. You deal with the director

    Issues escalate directly to director Karan Sood, not through layers of area managers. The person who signs the agreement answers for the building.

  3. Evidence you can forward

    Every open item is tracked on a live works register. Contractor sign ins are recorded digitally through SiteCheck, our own contractor sign in and induction system, included at no additional cost. Reports arrive in writing, ready for your agenda pack.

  4. The record to point to

    JKFM holds both the building management and cleaning contracts at 42-48 Claremont Street, South Yarra: a 24 level residential tower with 111 apartments, 25+ specialist contractors coordinated, and more than 5,000 cleaning hours delivered since October 2023. See the flagship record.
