Coverage

What our cleaning covers.

Every common area in your building, cleaned to a documented standard with full accountability.

  1. Lobbies and foyers

    Entrance areas, reception spaces, and ground floor common areas kept presentable daily. Floors mopped, surfaces wiped, glass cleaned, and high touch points sanitised.

  2. Stairwells and corridors

    All floors, from basement to rooftop. Stairwells swept and mopped, corridor floors maintained, handrails wiped, and lighting fixtures kept clean and functional.

  3. Car parks

    Basement and ground level car parks swept, degreased, and kept free of debris. Line markings visible, drains cleared, and storage cage areas maintained.

  4. Bin rooms

    Waste and recycling areas cleaned, deodorised, and managed. Bins rotated for collection, spillage cleaned promptly, and pest attractants minimised through regular deep cleans.

  5. Windows and glass

    Internal and external glass in common areas, including entrance doors, lobby windows, mirrors, and balustrades. Streak free finishes maintained on a scheduled rotation.

  6. Common amenities

    Gym rooms, meeting spaces, rooftop terraces, laundry areas, and any other shared facilities cleaned and maintained to a consistent standard.

Accountability

How accountability works.

Every clean is documented and verified. No assumptions, no guesswork.

  1. Documented checklists

    Every cleaning shift runs off the day's scheduled task list on a documented checklist specific to your building. Each task is signed off as completed, creating a clear record of what was done and when.

  2. Site specific teams

    Your building is cleaned by a team assigned to it, not a roving crew. Cleaners hold current police checks and complete site specific safety induction before their first shift.

  3. Quality audits

    Regular quality inspections conducted by management. Results documented, issues flagged immediately and photographed where an issue warrants it, and corrective actions tracked to completion.

Schedule structure

Cleaning schedule structure.

A layered approach that keeps your building consistently clean, not just occasionally presentable.

  1. Regular cleans

    Scheduled daily and weekly cleaning of high traffic areas. Lobbies, corridors, stairwells, and bin rooms maintained on a consistent rotation so standards never slip.

  2. Deep cleans

    Monthly and quarterly intensive cleaning including car park degreasing, window washing, and detailed attention to areas that build up grime over time.

  3. Requests as they arise

    Spills, post event clean ups, move in and move out cleans, and urgent requests handled promptly. Logged, documented, and reported alongside regular cleaning records.
