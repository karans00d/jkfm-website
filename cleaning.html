Cleaning Services

Structured cleaning programs with documented accountability for strata buildings across Melbourne.

What Our Cleaning Covers

Every common area in your building, cleaned to a documented standard with full accountability.

Lobbies and Foyers

Entrance areas, reception spaces, and ground-floor common areas kept presentable daily. Floors mopped, surfaces wiped, glass cleaned, and high-touch points sanitised.

Stairwells and Corridors

All floors, from basement to rooftop. Stairwells swept and mopped, corridor floors maintained, handrails wiped, and lighting fixtures kept clean and functional.

Car Parks

Basement and ground-level car parks swept, degreased, and kept free of debris. Line markings visible, drains cleared, and storage cage areas maintained.

Bin Rooms

Waste and recycling areas cleaned, deodorised, and managed. Bins rotated for collection, spillage cleaned promptly, and pest attractants minimised through regular deep cleans.

Windows and Glass

Internal and external glass in common areas, including entrance doors, lobby windows, mirrors, and balustrades. Streak-free finishes maintained on a scheduled rotation.

Common Amenities

Gym rooms, meeting spaces, rooftop terraces, laundry areas, and any other shared facilities cleaned and maintained to a consistent standard.

How Accountability Works

Every clean is documented and verified. No assumptions, no guesswork.

Documented Checklists

Every cleaning shift runs off a detailed task checklist specific to your building. Each task is signed off as completed, creating a clear record of what was done and when.

Photo Evidence

Before and after photos taken during each clean. Visual proof that work has been completed to standard, available for committee review at any time.

Quality Audits

Regular unannounced quality inspections conducted by management. Results documented, issues flagged immediately, and corrective actions tracked to completion.

Cleaning Schedule Structure

A layered approach that keeps your building consistently clean, not just occasionally presentable.

Regular Cleans

Scheduled daily and weekly cleaning of high-traffic areas. Lobbies, corridors, stairwells, and bin rooms maintained on a consistent rotation so standards never slip.

Deep Cleans

Monthly and quarterly intensive cleaning including car park degreasing, window washing, high-pressure cleaning, carpet extraction, and detailed attention to areas that build up grime over time.

Ad-Hoc Requests

Spills, post-event clean-ups, move-in/move-out cleans, and urgent requests handled promptly. Logged, documented, and reported alongside regular cleaning records.

What Makes JKFM Cleaning Different

Most cleaning contracts focus on hours worked. We focus on outcomes delivered.

Built-In Accountability

Checklists, photo evidence, and sign-off processes are not optional extras. They are part of every clean. Your committee never has to wonder whether the work was actually done or ask for proof after the fact.

Full Documentation

Every clean generates a record. Task completion logs, photo evidence, quality audit results, and issue reports all filed and available. A complete audit trail from day one.

Active Quality Control

We do not wait for complaints to identify issues. Regular unannounced inspections, scored against defined criteria, ensure standards are maintained consistently across every shift and every area of your building.

Integrated with Building Management

Our cleaning team works alongside building management, not in isolation. Defects spotted during cleaning are logged and escalated. Contractor visits are coordinated. Nothing operates in a silo.
