What it covers

What a South Yarra tower needs from its building manager.

Managing an apartment tower takes more than arranging repairs. Lifts, car stackers, fire safety systems and basement pumps all need regular servicing, accurate records and steady attention. This is how JKFM covers each one at its flagship building.

  1. Contractors

    What the committee needs
    The right contractors, on site when needed, with their work checked.
    What we do
    We coordinate 25+ specialist contractors at 42-48 Claremont Street, covering fire, lifts, car stackers, electrical, plumbing, waterproofing, waste, pest, doors, access control, security and glazing. We arrange servicing, meet contractors on site and follow up outstanding work.

  2. Compliance

    What the committee needs
    Inspections, servicing and compliance records kept on track.
    What we do
    We manage essential safety measures, fire system servicing, OHS and regulatory tracking, and keep records so the committee can see what is done, what is outstanding and what needs a decision.

  3. Open items and defects

    What the committee needs
    A clear record of what needs attention and what is being done about it.
    What we do
    Every open item is tracked on a live works register, followed through to completion and reported to the committee in writing. We also manage builder defects on behalf of the owners corporation.

  4. Quotes and invoices

    What the committee needs
    Knowing what the owners corporation is paying for.
    What we do
    Every contractor signs in digitally on arrival. Visits are met on site and compared against the invoice, and quotes are questioned before they are recommended.

  5. Residents

    What the committee needs
    A reliable point of contact for everyday building matters.
    What we do
    Resident communications, common property inspections and defect logging. Being on site helps us find issues early.

  6. Emergencies

    What the committee needs
    Support when something goes wrong outside business hours.
    What we do
    24/7 emergency response.

Terms

Straightforward terms for owners corporations.

Committees should know exactly what they are agreeing to before they appoint a building manager.

Six month trial

Every JKFM building management agreement starts with a six month trial period.

Your committee judges the work before the term means anything.

No security interests

JKFM does not take security interests over owners corporation assets or funds. Some service contracts in the market include PPSR registration rights, break fees and penalty interest; committees should check theirs.

Written reports

A written report to every committee meeting covering completed work, open items, upcoming maintenance, identified risks and financial summaries.

Who you deal with

The director, not an area manager.

Your building is managed by someone accountable for the outcome. Issues escalate directly to the director, not through layers of area managers.

At 42-48 Claremont Street, JKFM holds both the building management and cleaning contracts. The owners corporation has one accountable provider for both, with clear responsibility for standards and follow up.
