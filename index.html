The position

The challenge, and the JKFM solution.

  1. 01.1The challenge

    Most owners corporations deal with the same recurring issues.

    • a.Issues found late, leading to higher repair costs and avoidable disputes
    • b.Critical building data scattered across emails, spreadsheets, and contractor notes
    • c.Committees left driving outcomes instead of making informed decisions

  2. 01.2The JKFM solution

    JKFM brings structure and accountability through written records and direct oversight.

    • a.Written status updates so you have clear visibility of all open items
    • b.Risk identification to catch issues early, before they become costly
    • c.Contractor progress tracking with documented follow ups and quality checks
    • d.Retained decision authority so committees stay informed and in control

05

Method

Our 4 step management lifecycle.

A clear, repeatable process that keeps your building running and your committee informed.

  1. 05.1Understand

    We audit your building's current state, existing contracts, maintenance history, and compliance requirements.

  2. 05.2Plan

    We create a structured maintenance plan with priorities, timelines, and budget recommendations for committee approval.

  3. 05.3Manage

    We coordinate contractors, supervise work, conduct quality checks, and manage day to day building operations.

  4. 05.4Report

    Committees receive clear written reports covering completed work, open items, upcoming maintenance, and financial status.
